# Module 11 – AI in Manufacturing Industry

## Overview
This case study examines how AI is used in manufacturing, focusing on predictive maintenance and proposing an AI-based energy optimization system.

## Objective
The goal is to analyze how AI improves efficiency and decision-making in manufacturing while identifying challenges in implementation.

## Contents
- A11_CourtneyBernard_ITAI2372.docx

## Key Topics
- Predictive maintenance (Siemens case study)
- Machine learning and sensor data
- Equipment failure prevention
- Energy optimization proposal
- Efficiency and cost reduction

## Conclusion
AI significantly improves manufacturing operations, but challenges such as cost, data quality, and system integration must be addressed for successful implementation.
