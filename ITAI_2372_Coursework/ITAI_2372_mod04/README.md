# Assignment A04 – AI-Based Stock News Sentiment Tracker

## Overview
This project focuses on building an AI-Based Stock News Sentiment Tracker that analyzes financial news and determines whether the overall sentiment for a company is positive, negative, or neutral. The system uses Yahoo Finance as the data source and focuses on major technology companies.

## Objective
The goal of this assignment is to demonstrate how artificial intelligence can be used to simplify financial decision-making by automatically collecting, analyzing, and summarizing news sentiment.

## Problem Statement
Investors rely heavily on financial news, but there is too much content to read manually. Missing important updates can lead to poor decisions. This project addresses the need for an automated system that summarizes news sentiment quickly and efficiently.

## System Design
The system works in three main steps:

1. **Collect News**
   - Pulls headlines and summaries from Yahoo Finance
   - Focuses on five major tech companies

2. **Analyze Sentiment**
   - Classifies news as positive, negative, or neutral

3. **Generate Insights**
   - Summarizes daily sentiment
   - Highlights key reasons behind the sentiment

## Companies Analyzed
- Microsoft (MSFT)
- Google (GOOGL)
- Apple (AAPL)
- Tesla (TSLA)
- Meta (META)

## Example Results

- **Microsoft (MSFT): Positive**
  - Driven by strong cloud revenue and growth

- **Google (GOOGL): Neutral**
  - Focused on AI updates without major impact

- **Apple (AAPL): Positive**
  - Strong iPhone sales and App Store growth

- **Tesla (TSLA): Negative**
  - Production delays and stock decline

- **Meta (META): Neutral**
  - Stable updates and VR feature announcements

## Benefits
- Saves time by reducing the need to read multiple articles
- Simplifies complex financial information
- Helps support better investment decisions
- Enables continuous monitoring of multiple companies

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how AI can be used to analyze financial news and provide meaningful insights. By automating sentiment analysis, the system helps investors stay informed and make faster, more data-driven decisions.
