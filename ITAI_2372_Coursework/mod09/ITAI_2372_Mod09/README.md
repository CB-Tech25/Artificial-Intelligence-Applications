# Module 09 – Ethical and Societal Implications of AI

## Overview
This assignment explores the ethical and societal impact of artificial intelligence, focusing on social media recommendation algorithms. These systems influence what users see online and shape behavior, often without users being fully aware.

## Objective
The goal of this assignment is to analyze how AI systems affect society, identify ethical concerns, and propose solutions to improve transparency, fairness, and user control.

## Contents
- A09_EthicalCaseStudy_CourtneyBernard_ITAI2372.docx

## Key Topics
- Recommendation algorithms and personalization
- Bias and fairness in AI systems
- Privacy and data collection
- Manipulation and misinformation
- Societal and cultural impact

## Conclusion
AI recommendation systems provide efficiency and personalization, but they also raise serious concerns about transparency, bias, and user influence. Responsible AI development is necessary to ensure fairness and trust.
