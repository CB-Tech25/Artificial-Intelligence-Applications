# Module 12 – Advanced AI and AGI Applications

## Overview
This module explores advanced artificial intelligence concepts, focusing on Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and real-world applications in manufacturing and space systems. It highlights how AI can move beyond task-specific systems to more adaptive, decision-making systems.

## Assignments Included
- A12: AGI in Manufacturing Case Study
- NASA Space Mission AI Project (Proposal + Presentation)

## Objective
The goal of this module is to understand how advanced AI systems can improve efficiency, decision-making, and system-wide optimization while also evaluating the risks and ethical concerns of these technologies.

## Key Topics
- Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)
- Predictive maintenance systems
- Energy optimization using AI
- AI in high-risk environments (space missions)
- Human-AI collaboration
- Ethical considerations and system risks

## Structure
Each assignment is organized into its own section and includes:

- Assignment deliverables (documents and/or presentation)
