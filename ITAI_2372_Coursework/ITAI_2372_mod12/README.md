# Module 12 – AGI in Manufacturing

## Overview
This assignment explores how Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) could transform manufacturing by improving decision-making across multiple systems.

## Objective
The goal is to evaluate how AGI differs from traditional AI and how it could enhance efficiency, adaptability, and system-wide optimization.

## Contents

## Key Topics
- AGI vs traditional AI
- Predictive maintenance improvements
- Energy optimization
- Supply chain management
- Human-AI collaboration

## Risks and Challenges
- Job displacement
- Data security concerns
- Over-reliance on AI systems
- Ethical considerations

## Conclusion
AGI has the potential to revolutionize manufacturing, but must be implemented responsibly with human oversight and strong data management.
