# Module 10 – AI in Government and Public Sector

## Overview
This assignment analyzes how artificial intelligence is used in government systems, particularly in fraud detection, and proposes improvements to make these systems more user-friendly and fair.

## Objective
The goal is to evaluate both the benefits and limitations of AI in government and explore how AI can be used to support citizens while maintaining efficiency.

## Contents
- A10_Advantages_of_using_AI_CourtneyBernard_ITAI2372.docx

## Key Topics
- AI in fraud detection
- Efficiency and automation
- False positives and bias
- Transparency issues
- Human-centered AI systems

## Proposal
This assignment introduces an AI system that combines fraud detection with real-time user support, improving accessibility and reducing errors.

## Conclusion
AI improves efficiency in government systems, but must be balanced with fairness, transparency, and user support to build trust and accessibility.
