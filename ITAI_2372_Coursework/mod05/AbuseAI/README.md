# Abuse of AI – Case Study

## Overview
This assignment explores how artificial intelligence (AI) can be misused across different industries. While AI has many benefits, it can also be abused in ways that create serious ethical, social, and security risks.

## Objective
The goal of this assignment is to analyze the negative impacts of AI misuse, understand the risks involved, and evaluate possible solutions to prevent harmful outcomes.

## Contents
- Abuse_of_AI_CaseStudy_CourtneyBernard.docx (Written report)
- Abuse_of_AI_Presentation.pdf / .pptx (Presentation slides)

## Key Focus Areas

### AI Misuse
- Deepfakes and misinformation
- Biased decision-making systems
- Manipulation of data and automation tools
- AI used for fraud or cybercrime

### Ethical Concerns
- Lack of transparency in AI decisions
- Unfair treatment of individuals or groups
- Privacy violations
- Loss of human control over automated systems

### Real-World Impact
- Spread of misinformation
- Financial and identity fraud
- Social and economic inequality
- Loss of public trust in technology

### Risks
- Algorithmic bias
- Security vulnerabilities
- Lack of regulation
- Over-reliance on AI systems

### Potential Solutions
- Stronger regulations and policies
- Ethical AI development practices
- Human oversight (human-in-the-loop)
- Transparency and explainability in AI systems
- Regular testing and auditing of AI models

## Reflection
This assignment helped me understand that AI is not always positive and that its misuse can have serious consequences. It reinforced the importance of responsible AI development and the need for ethical guidelines when implementing AI systems.

## Conclusion
AI is a powerful tool, but it must be used responsibly. Preventing the abuse of AI requires a balance between innovation and regulation, along with continuous monitoring and ethical decision-making.
